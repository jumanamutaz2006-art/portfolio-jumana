Project details

Colleague: College Guidance Platform

A web platform that helps Egyptian high school students choose the right college, with everything they need to compare options in one place.

← Back to Projects

What it is

Overview

Colleague is a web platform for Egyptian high school students who are choosing a college. Instead of searching many different websites, students can find the information they need in one place and compare colleges that fit their academic track: Scientific, Literary, or Mathematics.

It was a team project. I worked on it with Yazed and our teammates.

How it works

The Project at a Glance

Problem

Scattered Information

College details are spread across different websites and sources, so comparing options takes a lot of time and effort.

Features

What It Offers

  • Colleges organized by academic track
  • A short guide to each major with a Learn More button
  • Requirements, study programs, and departments
  • Tools students may need during their studies
  • An About Us page and a Login button
