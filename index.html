Hello! I am a student from Cairo, Egypt, studying Business Information Systems at Capital University. I enjoy turning ideas into working software and learning how technology solves real business problems.

I joined SCCI as a participant, first as a member of the Devology team and later as its team leader. Together we built "Halo", a smart home-safety car website, and won first place at the SCCI Conference. I also worked with other teammates on "Colleague", a college guidance platform for Egyptian high school students.

Now I am a third-year student and an IT member at SCCI. I am no longer a participant: I am working on the SCCI website system, building both its front-end and its back-end.

In August 2026 I also completed a one-month internship at Crédit Agricole Egypt, in the City Stars branch.

I also completed two Innovation Area tracks in 2026: Programming and Artificial Intelligence, and Public Relations and Soft Skills. Right now I am focused on growing my web development and data skills.
